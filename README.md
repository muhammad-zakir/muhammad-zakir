## About Me

Full-stack engineer with 8+ years of experience, leaning backend. These days it's TypeScript across NestJS, Next.js and SvelteKit, shipped to self-hosted Kubernetes; before that, years of PHP (CodeIgniter, Laravel), Node.js (Adonis, Fastify), Vue, and a bit of Python. I care most about the parts that let a team ship safely: database design, continuous integration, infrastructure, and the conventions everyone works from.

Outside work I build small, free, privacy-first applications under [Itrium](https://itrium.id), which got me writing Rust.

## Projects

Free and open source, from [Itrium](https://github.com/itriumid):

- **[Honk](https://github.com/itriumid/honk)**: a cross-platform soundboard with global hotkeys and a macOS menu bar popover (Rust, Tauri, SvelteKit).
- **[Hindsight](https://github.com/itriumid/hindsight)**: keeps the last few minutes of what was said, up to three hours, in memory, and saves a clip on a hotkey. Nothing leaves your computer.
- **[Palettes](https://github.com/itriumid/palettes)**: the shared color palettes behind those applications, held to WCAG AA by a contrast check before every release.
- **[Agent Handbook](https://github.com/itriumid/agent-handbook)**: the conventions every AI coding agent follows across these projects, enforced through rulesets and pull request checks.
